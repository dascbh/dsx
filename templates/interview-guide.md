## 1. Opening (3 min)
> Thank you for your time. We want to understand how you <activity>; we are not going to evaluate you or sell you anything.
> With your permission, I will record <audio/video> only so we do not miss details. Access: <...>. We keep it for <period>. <N colleagues are listening.>
> You can skip any question or stop whenever you want. Your compensation is guaranteed.

## 2. Warm-up (5 min)
<!-- Easy to answer; builds context. -->
- What is your role/routine regarding <topic>?
- Which tools or people are part of it?

## 3. How you do it today (10 min)
- How do you <solve X> today?
- What else do you use? <!-- spreadsheet, paper, another person, "I don't do it" are alternatives too -->

## 4. Concrete episode (20–25 min)
<!-- Anchor on the LAST real episode. For JTBD, rebuild the timeline and the 4 forces. -->
- "Tell me about the last time <event>."
  - When was it? Where were you? What triggered it?
  - What did you do first? And then?
  - What did you try that did not work?
  - How did you decide? What almost made you give up?
  - How did you feel? What happened afterwards?

## 5. Closing (3 min)
- "Is there anything I did not ask that would be important?"
- "May I contact you again if a question comes up?"

## Neutral follow-ups
- "Can you give an example?" · "What happened next?" · "Why was that important?" · (silence)
